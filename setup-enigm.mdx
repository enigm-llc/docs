This guide explains the first-time setup flow for Enigm through Enigm Command at `https://app.enigm.io`. The flow creates the account, selects a subscription plan, completes payment, creates the account PIN, saves the recovery words, and opens the account-ready state.

The screenshots show Enigm Command with example values. Do not use public examples as real credentials, PINs, payment information, or recovery material.

## What You Need

Before starting, confirm that you have:

- Access to `https://app.enigm.io`.
- A username you want to use for your Enigm account.
- A password that meets the account requirements.
- A supported payment method.
- A private place to save your recovery words.

Account creation is performed through Enigm Command. Enigm App uses the resulting account identity for app access, Device Trust, secure messaging, secure calls, and related Enigm workflows.

## Setup Flow

Follow the product prompts in order. Do not skip the recovery-word step; recovery words are required if you later need to recover access.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start registration">
    Open `https://app.enigm.io`. If you do not already have an account, select **Sign up** from the sign-in screen.

    Existing users can sign in from this screen instead of starting registration.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create your account">
    Enter the username and password for the new account, repeat the password, review the terms and privacy links, and continue.

    The username is part of the account identity. Choose it carefully and avoid placing sensitive personal information in the username unless your use case requires it.

    Passwords must include:

    - An uppercase letter.
    - A lowercase letter.
    - A special character.
    - At least 12 characters.

    <div className="enigm-doc-image-frame">
      <img className="enigm-doc-image" src="/assets/images/command/setup-enigm-02-create-account.png" alt="Enigm Command account creation form with username, password, terms, and continue action" />
    </div>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose a plan">
    Review the available plan options and billing terms shown in Enigm Command. Select the plan that matches your intended use, then continue.

    The plan screen can show annual and monthly options. Current prices, taxes, discounts, supported payment methods, and billing terms should be confirmed in Enigm Command before continuing.

    <div className="enigm-doc-image-frame">
      <img className="enigm-doc-image" src="/assets/images/command/setup-enigm-03-plan-selection.png" alt="Enigm Command subscription plan selection screen" />
    </div>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Complete payment">
    Select the available payment method you want to use. Enigm Command can present card payment, cryptocurrency payment, and Code Coin payment-code options depending on current availability and account eligibility.

    Payment-method availability, processing requirements, taxes, and final amounts can vary by region, time, payment method, and commercial terms. Review the product screen before confirming payment.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create your PIN">
    After payment, create the account PIN requested by the setup flow. The PIN protects account access and sensitive account workflows after authentication.

    Use a PIN that you can remember and do not share it with other people.

    <div className="enigm-doc-image-frame">
      <img className="enigm-doc-image" src="/assets/images/command/setup-enigm-04-create-pin.png" alt="Enigm account setup screen for creating a PIN" />
    </div>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save your recovery words">
    Enigm shows recovery words during account setup. Save them in a private, durable place before continuing.

    Recovery words are sensitive. Anyone who obtains them may be able to take control of the account. Enigm cannot recover them for you if you lose them.

    <Warning>
      Do not store recovery words in screenshots, shared notes, chat messages, email drafts, cloud folders, or other places that could expose them to another person or service.
    </Warning>

    <div className="enigm-doc-image-frame">
      <img className="enigm-doc-image" src="/assets/images/command/setup-enigm-05-recovery-words.png" alt="Enigm account setup screen for saving recovery words" />
    </div>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Finish setup">
    When the account-ready screen appears, the initial account setup is complete.

    From this screen, you can continue to Enigm Command or download the Enigm application when available for your platform and deployment.

    <div className="enigm-doc-image-frame">
      <img className="enigm-doc-image" src="/assets/images/command/setup-enigm-06-account-ready.png" alt="Enigm account ready screen with actions to open Enigm Command or download the app" />
    </div>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## After Setup

After the account is created, use Enigm Command to manage account lifecycle, products, payment state, devices, sessions, Enigm eSIM, Enigm Server, Enigm Link, Enigm Key, and supported administrative workflows.

Use Enigm App for private messaging, secure calls, Device Trust workflows, PIN-gated app access, network privacy controls, and account-associated app usage.

## Security Notes

- Keep your password, PIN, and recovery words private.
- Save recovery words before leaving the recovery step.
- Do not share screenshots that reveal account identifiers, payment details, recovery words, or other sensitive account material.
- Review terms, privacy information, plan terms, taxes, and final payment amount before confirming.
- Sign out on shared or untrusted devices.

See [App Account Model](/app/account-model), [Command Product Lifecycle](/command/product-lifecycle), and [Platform Limitations](/legal/limitations).
